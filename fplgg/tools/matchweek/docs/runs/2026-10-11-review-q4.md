## Findings

1. **The Matchup list's player name breaks in the middle of a word at 390 px in the runner's Chromium.** The fix in
   `20-matchday.css` (`.md-hc b`: a two-line clamp with `overflow-wrap:anywhere`, the centre column 80 px) trades the
   cut "Donnarum..." for "Donnarumm" / "a" on two lines: the published Chromium screens show it in both states
   (`01-finding-matchup-list-chromium-390.jpg`, from `preview/shots/snap/chromium/03-matchup-1-list.jpg`), while WebKit
   fits the name on one line (`02-matchup-list-webkit-390.jpg`). The run report's verification line ("the Matchup list:
   the name and its meta line on one line each") is true of the builder's own Chromium, whose fonts are narrower, and not
   of the gate's; in this environment the same build at 360 px breaks the same name (`.md-hc b` 31 px tall, two lines,
   width 79 = scrollWidth 79). A surname is one word: Parker's two-line rule is for team names. What to do: keep a word
   whole (`overflow-wrap:normal`, `word-break:keep-all`) and give the name the room it needs on the runner's fonts, at 390
   and 360 px (the centre column narrower, or a smaller size for a long name, or the meta line under the name taking the
   space), checked against the longest surnames in the league's rosters (Donnarumma, Branthwaite, Gibbs-White,
   J.Rak-Sakyi); add the case to `tests/app-ui.js` or `tests/app-gallery.js`; and consider a gallery rule for a single
   word rendered on two lines, since the clipped-text rule cannot see this. Verify on the published gallery, not a local
   run: the fonts differ.
2. **The gallery photographs screen 01 in different states in the two browsers.** Chromium's "matchday overview" shows
   the page nobody follows (the gear icon, "Match of the week", "Pick your team to make this page yours"), WebKit's shows
   the followed team ("Your matchup", the crest, "Callum · you"), in both states (`03-finding-screen01-chromium.jpg`,
   `04-screen01-webkit.jpg`). The cause is in `gallery.js`: after `page.goto(base)` it writes `emt-myteam` to
   localStorage and then `page.goto(base + '#/matchday/overview')`, which in Chromium is a same-document navigation (a
   window flag set before it survives it; checked here), so the app never re-reads the followed team until the next
   route change; WebKit reloads on it. A reviewer scrolling the index sees the two browsers disagree on the first screen
   for a reason that has nothing to do with the browsers. Fix: reload after writing localStorage (`page.reload()`, or
   set it with `context.addInitScript` before the first load), and a line in `tests/app-gallery.js` that the walker
   reloads after following the team.
3. **At 360 px the clipped-text rule finds 15 cuts, and the invariant is both widths.** The gallery at `--width 360` in
   Chromium on the committed snapshot, both states: Search, the result name `b.ell` "J.Rak-Sakyi" cut by 23 px
   (`05-finding-search-360.jpg`); League > Stats, the leader line "Owen Whitlock · 2nd" by 2 px; Feed > League, the
   auto-pick panel's team cell "Wirtz Case Scenario" by 8 px (a team name, Parker's never-cut rule;
   `06-finding-feed-league-360.jpg`) and the derby card's `b.f-cond` "The Sacred Heart Derby" by 3 px; Matchday overview,
   the mini auto-pick strip "TEAM SHEETS" by 4 px; Feed > For you and Messages, the thread rows "Start a rumour. Archizio
   never names a source." by 23 px and "Wants a quote before The Sacred Heart Derby" by 20 px. BUGS.md #44 (on the
   branch) records six of these from the snapshot state and parks them "for a later run"; the Q5 review of 10 Oct failed
   on one such cut, and the review invariant the manager set is no clipped text at 360 and 390 px. What to do: the names
   and the team names wrap or get room (as the League table and the Seasons table do); the thread rows and the leader

   then the workflows run the gallery at 360 px as well as 390 px (a second pass or a `--widths` flag), so the gate holds
   the invariant at both widths; close #44 with the hash.
