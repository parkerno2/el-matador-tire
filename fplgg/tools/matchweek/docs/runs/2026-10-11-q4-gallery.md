## The request
Parker, 10 Oct 2026 (Q4, lane app): "the scheduled agents seem sloppy, we need a better way to oversee them." In the
Preview and Build workflows, before anything is published: a screenshot gallery (every page and sub-tab, a player
sheet, a manager sheet and the menu, at 390 px, in Chromium AND WebKit, on the demo snapshot, before kick-off and live
where the data allows; committed with the preview as `preview/shots/` with an `index.html` that shows them in order);

## What changed (the `preview` branch)
- `fplgg/tools/gallery/gallery.js` (new): opens the built demo at 390 px in headless Chromium and WebKit, in two states
  where the data allows (the page's clock 2 hours before the gameweek's deadline, and the snapshot's own time), walks
  the contrast audit's 42 screens (every page and sub-tab, the four matchups, the player, manager, menu, How it works,
  search, club identity and Feed post sheets), photographs each (JPEG, 1x) and checks it: no player code twice across
  the Matchup pitch, list and bench, the Lineup pitch, bench and list, the manager sheet's pitch and bench; no NaN,
  undefined or null as a word; no horizontal overflow of the page or the open sheet; no clipped text on a primary line
  (a secondary line, class `sub`, may give way and is noted); zero console and page errors; no request for an EA asset;
  no em dash and no en dash in prose (an en dash inside a figure is BUGS.md #43 and is noted until Parker decides); a
  page or sheet that did not render. Writes `OUT/<state>/<browser>/NN-screen.jpg`, `OUT/index.html` (one page: the
  build and the verdict on top, the findings, then every screen in order with the browsers side by side) and
  `OUT/report.json`; prints the readable list and exits 1 on any finding.
- `fplgg/tools/gallery/lib.js` (new): the pure rules (the words, the dash rule, the duplicate rule, the list, the index
  page). `fplgg/tools/gallery/README.md`: what each check fails and notes, how to run it, the CI wiring.

  index page, the walker's screens and checks, both workflows' wiring, the docs. `tests/app-contrast.js` C8 follows the
