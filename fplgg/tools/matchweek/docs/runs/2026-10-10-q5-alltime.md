## What changed
- `fplgg/tools/matchweek/history/2025-26.json`: the season exactly as given, one line of UTF-8 (league 47320, six managers by code, the standings, 38 weekly scores each, the 114 fixtures, the all-play records, the all-stars, the drafts).
- `fplgg/tools/matchweek/tools/history.js`: loads `history/*.json`, runs the three checks the export was made with (each manager's weekly scores sum to his PF, the fixtures reproduce every W, D, L, PF and PA, and the head-to-heads of all seasons equal `league.json` `seeded`) plus the shape checks (ranks 1 to n, Pts = 3 W + D, the fixtures agree with the weekly scores, the all-play adds up), writes the `const HISTORY=[...]` header, and anonymises the seasons for the demo (codes and team names through the demo's mapping, the source line and the league id dropped).
- `ci-build.sh`, `fplgg/tools/preview/build-preview.js`, `fplgg/tools/demo/build-demo.js`: the HISTORY header after the LEAGUE header in `core.js`; a history that fails its checks fails the build.
- `src/alltime.js`: the model, keyed by manager code (league.json `ini`), never by team name. The seasons are the HISTORY files plus this season from the finished H2H Fixtures rows (the standings worked out from them: 3 a win, 1 a draw, points for breaks ties; complete once every fixture is finished). Per manager: seasons played, titles (league winners of a complete season; 2025/26: PN), the finish by season with the team name that season (EG's Maize 'n' Mount), P, W, D, L, league points, points per match, win %, points for and against, the average score a gameweek, the highest and lowest gameweek score (season, GW, opponent), the biggest win and heaviest defeat (season, GW, opponent, the scores), the longest winning and losing runs (runs cross seasons; where they ran), the all-play record (the file's plus this season's finished gameweeks against every other score), the record against each other manager (W, D, L, for, against), his 2025/26 first pick and best player. The head-to-heads are SEED plus this season's finished meetings, the same sum the Derbies page shows. Memoised per data load.
- `src/pages/league/alltime.js` and `src/pages/league.js`: League > All-time. One table: #, crest, Manager (the first name, a trophy per title beside it with its count in the label, the seasons under it), P, W-D-L, Pts, PPM, Avg; a tap on P, W-D-L, Pts, PPM or Avg sorts by it, a second tap flips it (kept in localStorage); every row opens the manager sheet; your row carries the bar and tint the main table uses. Under it four record tiles (the highest score, the biggest win, the longest winning and losing runs, with the holder and where), then the data-state line "Finished gameweeks only."
- `src/sheets/manager.js`: the All-time tab after Season. His line (played, W-D-L, points, per match, for, against, a gameweek, won), Records (the seven rows), Seasons (newest first, the finish starred while this season runs, the title marked), the 2025/26 draft (first pick, best player with position, club and points), Head to head (a row per manager he has met, in all-time order, W-D-L and for-against, each opening that manager's sheet).
- `src/css/45-alltime.css`: the grids; the active sort header is the one purple accent; every figure on the dark card in the numeric font; hyphens only.
- `fplgg/tools/contrast/audit.js`: League > All-time and the sheet's All-time tab in the walk (42 screens now).
- `tests/app-alltime.js` (new, 46 checks): the file and its three checks (and that a slip fails them), the model reproducing 2025/26 exactly with no finished match this season (every line, the records worked out independently from the fixtures, the all-play recomputed from the weekly scores, the head-to-heads equal to seeded), the GW6 fixture added (totals, the finish so far, the Derbies agreement pair by pair, the all-play, a new manager's one season, a record this season beating the file), the page (the rows and their order, the sorts, the records, the labels, no dash and no hole), the sheet's panel (the line, the records, the seasons, the draft, the head to head, the alias season, the empty state), the builds and the audit. `tests/app-contrast.js` (the audit's lists), `tests/app-demo.js` and `tests/app-league.js` (the builds' lines) follow.
