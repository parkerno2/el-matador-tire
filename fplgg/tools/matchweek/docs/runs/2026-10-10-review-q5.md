## Findings
1. **FAIL. The manager sheet's Seasons table cuts a long team name at 360 px.** Screen: a manager sheet, All-time tab,
   the Seasons card, at 360 px on the live data (`01-finding-sheet-seasons-360.png`): "The Soaring Gulls" reads
   "The Soaring G..." on both rows (the demo's "Wirtz Case Scenario" too; "Kobbie Mainoo Fan" is cut the same way on
   Baha's sheet). The Team column of `.at-sr` is `minmax(0,1fr)` beside fixed columns of 56, 44, 52 and 32 px, which
   leaves about 102 px at 360 px against the 107 px the name needs; at 390 px it fits (132 px). The review invariant
   (10 Oct) is no clipped text at 360 and 390 px, and Parker's rule for the League table (Q6) is that a team name may
   take two lines and is never cut. What would fix it: let the team name wrap to two lines in that cell (drop `ell`,
   allow the row to grow), as the Q6 table does; or shorten the fixed columns at 360 px.
