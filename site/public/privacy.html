Privacy Policy

Effective 8 October 2026

The short version

  • Matchweek is a free companion app for FPL Draft leagues, in private beta.
  • We use your league's public FPL Draft data plus whatever you choose to add in the app: a PIN, a profile, quotes, reactions, votes, rumours and predictions.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking cookies. We never sell or rent your data.
  • What you post is visible to the other managers in your league.
  • Email hello@matchweek.gg to see, fix or delete anything about you.

1. Who we are

Matchweek ("we", "us") runs the Matchweek app and matchweek.gg. Matchweek is an unofficial companion app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to the Premier League, Fantasy Premier League, FPL Draft or any club or player.

2. What we collect

League data from FPL Draft

When a league uses Matchweek, we read that league's data from the public FPL Draft service: team names, the manager names shown there, squads, lineups, transactions, scores and fixtures. We store it in the league's data sheet so the app can show tables, matchups, recaps and history.

What you add

  • Claiming your team. You choose a PIN. We keep only a one-way hash of it, never the PIN itself. Your phone stores a sign-in token in its local storage so you stay signed in.
  • Your profile (optional). A display name, colours and a photo if you add one.
  • Social features. Press-room quotes, reactions, poll votes, rumours and on-the-record predictions, with your team name and the time you posted.
  • The waitlist (optional). If you join the waitlist on matchweek.gg, the email address and any answers you give, collected through Tally.

Stored on your device

The app saves a few small settings on your phone or computer (local storage, not cookies): your sign-in token, your chosen team, display preferences and which posts you've seen. It also caches the app so it opens quickly. Clearing your browser's site data removes all of this.

What we don't collect

We don't ask you to sign in with Google, Apple or anyone else, and we can't see your FPL password. We don't collect your location or contacts, and we don't run analytics or advertising trackers.

3. How we use it

  • To run the app: tables, matchups, projections, recaps, the feed and the social features.
  • To make AI-written content for your league, such as feed posts, recaps and the narrated Gameweek Show. These are built from league data and what managers post in the app. See section 5.
  • To keep things working and safe: preventing spam, enforcing the Terms and fixing bugs.
  • To reply when you contact us, and to tell waitlist members when Matchweek opens up.

Our legal bases, where the law asks for them: providing the service you asked for, our legitimate interest in running and improving a league app, and your consent for optional things like the waitlist and your profile photo. You can withdraw consent at any time.

4. Who can see it

Everything you post in the app (quotes, reactions, votes, rumours, predictions and your profile) is visible to the other managers in your league. League data is visible to members of that league. Recaps and previews may be published as web pages that anyone with the link can open. They use team names and managers' first names.

6. How long we keep it

League data and posts are kept for the season and in the league's archive, so history and head-to-head records keep working. If you leave a league or ask us to, we delete or anonymise what's tied to you. Some records may stay in backups for up to 90 days. Waitlist entries are kept until Matchweek opens to you or you ask us to remove them.

7. Your choices and rights

You can ask to see, correct, download or delete your data, or object to how we use it. Email hello@matchweek.gg from the address you used, or tell us your league and team so we can confirm it's you. Depending on where you live (for example the UK, the EU or California), you may also have the right to complain to your data protection regulator. We don't sell personal information and don't use it for targeted advertising.

8. Security

PINs are hashed, connections use HTTPS, and only league members can post. No service is perfectly secure. Use a PIN you don't use anywhere else.

9. Children

Matchweek isn't directed at children under 13 (under 16 in the UK and EU), and we don't knowingly collect their data. If you think a child has used Matchweek, contact us and we'll delete it.

10. Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we'll also say so in the app.
