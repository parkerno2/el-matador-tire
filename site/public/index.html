Matchweek features

Live Scoring

Every head-to-head scores live from kickoff: goals, assists, clean sheets and bonus, with your derby names on the marquee.

Dynamic OVRs

Ratings rise on hot streaks and drop in cold spells. Haul 10-plus points and the card goes black and gold: Team of the Week.

Projected Points

Forecasts before kickoff, a projected final while games run, and predicted auto-subs applied before the official game catches up.

Expected Points & Luck Index

See what every performance deserved. Won 41-38 but should have lost? Provable, and on permanent record.

Pot Tracker

The pot, the payouts and the Manager of the Month races, tracked in one place all season. No spreadsheet required.

Trades & Waiver Monitoring

Every pickup, drop, trade and denied claim in a live feed, next to manager profiles and an analysis lab for settling debates.

How Matchweek works

Matchweek connects directly to your FPL Draft league and updates automatically: results, points, trades, waivers and every move of the table, all season long. Commissioners customize the rest, from derby names and team colors to the pot, the payouts and the prize calendar, so the app feels like it was built for your league. Because it was.
