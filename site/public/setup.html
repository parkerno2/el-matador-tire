Connect your FPL Draft league

Matchweek reads the league straight from FPL — team names, managers and rosters come from there. You only decide how it looks and what it plays for.

Find it in the address bar on draft.premierleague.com while viewing your league. The draft must be complete.

No lookup service yet? Use Paste JSON: open https://draft.premierleague.com/api/league/<id>/details in a new tab, select all, copy, paste.
