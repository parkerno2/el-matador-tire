# Voice profile pictures

The four fictional voices' avatars, used by `fplgg/tools/matchweek/src/feed/voices.js` (`pic()` / `avatar()`), 256 x 256 JPEG.
The app draws them in a circle over each voice's glyph; if a picture fails to load, the glyph shows instead.

| File | Voice | Picture |
|---|---|---|
| `archizio.jpg` | Archizio Poblano, the insider | On the phone in a stadium concourse at dusk, a knowing half smile |
| `clark.jpg` | Clark Moldridge, The Terrace | Fan-channel studio, ring light, headphones round his neck, a dead-eyed stare down the lens |
| `malcolm.jpg` | Malcolm Tyre, the booth | Commentary gantry at night, headset, overcoat, a dry smile |
| `jive.jpg` | Jive Tidlsey, assistant manager | Sunny training ground, plain training top, lanyard, clipboard |

Made 8 Oct 2026 with Google Gemini (image generation), approved by Parker the same day. Each one takes its framing from the
kind of profile picture its archetype uses (the insider on the phone, the streamer in his studio, the commentator in the
gantry, the coach on the training ground), with an original face: none is meant to resemble a real person, and none has
a club badge, logo or text. To replace one, keep the file name, 256 x 256, face centred and filling about two thirds of the frame.
